None of the situations below exist. Nobody is running any of them, no deployment is configured
anywhere in this repository, and nothing described here has been broadcast. They are here because
protocol rules are easier to hold on to when a person is attached to them.

Read them as thought experiments with the mechanics exposed underneath.

One mechanic runs under nearly all of them, so it is worth setting down before the stories start.
Recovery in Tandem is a REFUND, and a REFUND spends the carrier under the same 2 of 2 as everything
else, so both signatures are on it. The pair sign it while they are still on speaking terms. Its
input sequence of 52,560 blocks decides when it can confirm, never who can produce it, so once the
carrier is that old either of them can broadcast it without the other. Every confirmed MARK or
ROTATE replaces the carrier, which restarts the clock and retires the refund signed against the
outpoint that just disappeared, so a fresh one gets signed alongside each entry.

## People making something together

### A creative partnership

Two illustrators work on a book across three years and want the order of who contributed what to be
settled while it is happening rather than argued about afterwards.

CREATE founds the record. Each delivery is a MARK with kind `0x01`, image, or `0x00`, note. If they
finish, a CLOSE with reason `0x00`, mutual completion. If one of them walks away, the refund signed
alongside the last chapter becomes confirmable 52,560 blocks later, and whichever of them is still
paying attention broadcasts it. It pays them equally.

### An artist collaboration across a distance

A composer and a filmmaker who have never met in person exchange work for a year. There is no shared
employer, no contract, and no obvious jurisdiction between them.

Each exchange is a MARK, with kind `0x02`, audio, or `0x01`, image. Neither can add an entry alone,
because every MARK spends the carrier under the 2 of 2. If one of them changes their signing setup
partway through, a ROTATE handles it and the earlier chapters stay exactly where they are. The
recovery path needs no jurisdiction at all.

### A joint project with a defined end

Two people restore a building, run a season of something, or ship a piece of work with an agreed
finish line.

Milestones are MARKs with kind `0x03`. The finish is a CLOSE, which advances the sequence one last
time and pays both keys exactly equally. If the project dies quietly instead, nobody has to announce
it. The refund already signed against the last milestone waits out its 52,560 blocks, and then one
of them can end it without a conversation neither wants to have.

## People who share a life

### A couple

Two people want a record of their own that is not held inside a service either of them can be locked
out of.

CREATE at the beginning, MARKs for the moments they choose to record, ROTATE when a phone is
replaced. Two endings are defined, and the protocol treats them the same way: a CLOSE with reason
`0x01`, relationship ended, pays both keys equally, and so does a refund broadcast after a year of
silence. There is no version of this where one party ends up with more of the carrier than the other.

### A family record

A parent and a child, or two siblings, keep a record spanning decades. Wallets will change several
times over that span.

ROTATE is the operation that makes decades plausible. Each rotation replaces the key pair, advances
the sequence by one, and preserves every chapter. The object key is derived from the founding
transaction, so it survives every change of hands between the two current holders. Recovery is
arranged in advance and re-signed after every entry, which over decades is a habit rather than an
event.

## People who share an obligation

### Co-founders

Two founders want the sequence of early decisions to be fixed before anybody's memory becomes
motivated.

Each decision is a MARK, most naturally kind `0x03`, milestone, or `0x00`, note. Because the sequence
must advance by exactly one, nothing can be quietly inserted later. If the company outgrows the
arrangement, a CLOSE with reason `0x02`, migrate outside Tandem, ends it without implying a falling
out.

### A shared commitment

Two people promise each other something with a schedule attached: a savings plan, a training plan, a
standing obligation.

Each fulfilment is a MARK. The refund is the interesting part here, because its clock restarts on
every confirmed MARK or ROTATE. An object that is being kept up with never reaches the point where
one side can broadcast the standing refund alone. An object that has gone quiet for 52,560 blocks
does.

### A long term agreement

Two parties record the amendments to an arrangement over many years, without either of them holding
the master copy.

Each amendment is a MARK whose commitment binds the document hash to that exact position in that
exact object. Neither party can produce a revision that appears earlier than it was, and neither can
remove one. If they later disagree about what was agreed, the argument is about content, not about
order.

## People who keep records

### A collector and a maker

A maker and the person who acquired a piece keep a joint provenance record, kept by both of them
rather than asserted by one.

CREATE founds it. Each exhibition, repair or condition report is a MARK with kind `0x04`, link, or
`0x05`, opaque data. Note the limit honestly: this records what the two of them agreed to record. It
does not prove the object in the world is the object in the photograph.

### A historical record kept by two parties

Two institutions, or two people on opposite sides of an account of events, keep one record that
neither can edit alone.

Every entry needs both signatures, which is exactly the point. A single institution's archive is a
claim. An entry that required a counterparty's signature is a different kind of artefact. If the
relationship ends, a CLOSE says so on the record, and the record stays readable afterwards.

## What the protocol thinks about all of this

Nothing. Its entire vocabulary of meaning is six MARK kinds and four CLOSE reasons.

| MARK kinds | CLOSE reasons |
|---|---|
| note, image, audio, milestone, link, opaque data | mutual completion, relationship ended, migrate outside Tandem, other |

There is no field for what an object is, no category, no type, and no place to say whether the two
keys belong to spouses or to shipping companies. That silence is deliberate. It is also why every
situation above is somebody's idea rather than a supported feature, and why none of them exist.

If a scenario here made you want to check whether the state you would be reading is trustworthy,
that instinct is correct, and [why two indexers](/index-tandem/discover/independent-verification/)
is the page for it.
